"Carnet de recette" is an app designed to replace paper recipe notebooks by offering a digital, centralized, and accessible solution.
Using a physical notebook has several limitations:
It can be lost, damaged, or soiled.
It is not always at hand when an idea or recipe needs to be jotted down quickly.
Recipes are often scattered across various sources, making them difficult to organize.
Quickly finding a specific recipe is difficult.
Sharing recipes is inconvenient.
The app aims to enable users to easily create, organize, find, and share their recipes from any device.
